# Portfolio

This context is Laxman’s public portfolio. It presents a canonical profile, authored work, and a chronological account of life and work.

## Language

**Site Profile**:
The canonical authored record of identity, social destinations, and professional experience presented across the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Personal data, user profile

**Published Document**:
An authored entry available in a public collection, consisting of descriptive metadata and a narrative body.
_Avoid_: Content item, page

**Writing**:
A published document whose primary purpose is an article or essay.
_Avoid_: Blog post, post

**Project**:
A published document that presents something built, including its purpose and relevant context.
_Avoid_: Case study, portfolio item

**Lifeline**:
The chronological account of personal, educational, professional, and project milestones.
_Avoid_: Timeline, career history

**Milestone**:
A dated point on the Lifeline representing one or more related events.
_Avoid_: Marker, entry
